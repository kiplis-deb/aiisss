How it works

From question to answer in three quiet steps.

  1. 01

    Pick a mode

    Each mode tunes the system prompt, temperature, and (when needed) the model.

  2. 02

    Add context

    Attach images or files, or just type — auto-resizing composer keeps you in flow.

  3. 03

    Stream the answer

    Tokens render as they arrive. Copy any reply, switch modes, or clear to start fresh.
